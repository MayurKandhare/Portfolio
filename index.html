Employee Attrition Analysis

HR Analytics

Problem

A company lost 231 of its 1,417 employees, which is 16.3%. HR wants to know where people are leaving from and which groups leave the most, so they can plan how to keep them.

Tools used

  • Power BI
  • DAX

What I analyzed

Employee exits by department, salary level, job role, age group, gender and years of experience, plus how satisfied people were.

Key numbers

16.3%attrition rate
231exits
1,417employees

Key insights

  • Most people leave early. Exits peak at around 1 year of experience.
  • Administration has the most exits (84), followed by Sales (52).
  • Sales loses about 1 in 4 employees (52 of 207). Operations, the biggest team, loses about 1 in 12 (43 of 512).

Marketing Campaign Success Analysis

Marketing Analytics

Problem

Companies spend money on marketing campaigns across different channels, regions and budgets. This project looks at which campaigns tend to succeed, so money can be spent where it works best.

Tools used

  • Power BI
  • DAX

What I analyzed

Campaign success by channel (Email, Radio, SMS, Social Media, TV), product category, region, quarter and budget, along with conversion rate.

Key numbers

59.6%success rate
250campaigns
149successful

Key insights

  • 149 of 250 campaigns (59.6%) were successful.
  • Radio and Email had the most successful campaigns. SMS had the fewest.
  • Higher budgets had more successful campaigns, with the most around $25K.

E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

Sales Analytics

Problem

An online store sells on Shopify, eBay and Amazon in several countries. The owner wants to know what sells best, where, and how much profit the business makes.

Tools used

  • Power BI
  • DAX

What I analyzed

Sales, profit and profit margin by platform, product category, product, country and month.

Key numbers

1.79Mtotal sales
32.84%profit margin
1,000orders

Key insights

  • The store made 1.79M in sales and 588K in profit, a 32.84% margin.
  • Electronics bring in about 59% of sales. Headphones, Laptop and Smartphone are the top sellers.
  • Shopify, eBay and Amazon earn similar revenue and similar profit margins.
